# Component coverage

Each component module maps to a working composition. Canonical implementations and app re-exports share a story; they are not separate libraries. Button stories also cover LightButton and ExpandableLightButton. Field appearance recipes are consumed by Input, Textarea, NativeSelect, SelectTrigger, and Button.
